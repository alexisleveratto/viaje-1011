Viaje F.M.

soft songs for late hours

What this is

Viaje F.M. is a small, self-hosted tool that runs an overnight music radio as a YouTube Live stream. Each night, at a scheduled time, it plays a playlist of licensed music from its own library and streams it to the owner's YouTube channel, then ends the stream in the morning.

How it uses YouTube

The app signs in to one YouTube account only: the channel owner's own account. It uses the YouTube Data API to create the nightly live broadcast and attach the video stream to it. It does not access any other person's account, and it is not offered to other users.

Your data

No data is shared with anyone or stored beyond the owner's own channel. The app does not collect information about viewers, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not sell or pass data to third parties. Read the full privacy policy.
